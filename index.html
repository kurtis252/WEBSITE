Hello, I'm Kurtis

I'm a videographer, motion designer, drone pilot and photographer based on the Norfolk/Suffolk border, working across East Anglia and always happy to travel for the right job.

I've worked on everything from weddings and festivals to esports, brand campaigns and broadcast, with a degree in VFX and Motion Graphics and a stack of qualifications in photography, film and media behind it. I can take a project from first idea to final edit, whether it's a product film, an animated explainer, a podcast, a portrait session or a live-streamed event.

I'm also a qualified teacher with a background in inclusive education, and experience developing elearning courses for adults and young people. So if your video needs to explain, train or inform, I make sure it looks good and I make sure people actually understand it.
